## Overview

The [[Dwendalian Empire]] is a dominant human-led nation located on the continent of [[Wildemount]], controlling the western half of [[Wynandir]].

It is a highly structured, law-driven monarchy focused on order, expansion, and centralized control.

- Capital: [[Rexxentrum]]
- Ruler: [[King Bertrand Dwendal]]
- Government Type: Absolute Monarchy
- Era Context: ~835 PD (during rising tensions with [[Kryn Dynasty]])

The Empire has spent generations expanding its territory and influence across Western Wynandir.
