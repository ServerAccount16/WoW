Across the Menagerie Coast and beyond, notices began appearing. Boards in cities, postings in taverns, even word passed hand to hand. The message was clear: [[Lady Sephrine Dalmore]] of the [[Clovis Concord]] was hiring.

The scale alone said enough. The sheer number of postings meant she had money. A lot of it.

She was calling for adventurers to explore a newly uncovered ruin of unknown origin, offering an unusually high payout. Artifacts, relics, documents, and especially survivors of a prior expedition would all be rewarded. Payment for relics was rumored to be one-to-one with their value. There were even whispers that the first group had been paid upwards of ten thousand gold just to enter.

That first group never came back.

---

We arrived at a wealthy manor to meet the rest of the expedition. There were around a hundred people in total, maybe half of them actual adventurers. It was immediately obvious that recruitment had not been selective. Anyone remotely capable had been accepted.

The party was not yet formed.  
[[Henrik]], [[Demingo Indigo|Demingo]], and [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] had come together.  
[[Orivon]] and [[Aporia]] had come together.

At some point, an official addressed the crowd and clarified the situation. The ruin had only recently been uncovered. A first expedition had gone in and vanished. Now we were being sent to do what they could not, and, if possible, find out what happened to them. With promise of extra pay if we bring them back alive.

We were split into groups of five to six and loaded into a series of eight carriages, several carrying supplies. From there, the entire expedition began moving northeast toward the Wuyun Gorge, though we cut north earlier than expected, approaching from the western side of the mountain ridge.

---

We made camp at the base of the mountain.

The expedition leader stood apart from the rest. His armor was well-used but meticulously maintained, bearing the mark of the [[Clovis Concord]]. He carried himself with confidence, clearly someone used to command.

He addressed us briefly. In the morning, we would ascend and enter the ruins. Another group had already found the entrance. We were warned not to steal artifacts, though anything turned in would be paid for. He stressed caution, not arrogance, and then dismissed us.

Despite the warning, the overall mood was far too relaxed. Most of the expedition treated this like a routine ruin crawl.

That night, [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] noticed [[Aporia]] praying before bed.  
At some point, we also realized her familiar was a winged cat, something neither [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] nor [[Henrik]] had ever seen before.

---

By morning, we ascended the mountain.

The entrance appeared as a massive carved opening, initially resembling a natural cavern. But the deeper we went, the more unnatural it became. There was a structure made of stone that was pale and impossibly precise, untouched by time. A faint blue light pulsed through it in steady intervals.

Outside was a camp that had clearly been abandoned in haste.

Inside, the passage split into three paths:

- Left sloped downward, with flickering light and a metallic scent
- Right opened wider, with angular supports and steadier light, showing signs of activity
- Center was smooth, etched, and felt… watchful

We chose the right path, along with two other groups.

---

After about fifteen minutes, the structure opened further. The walls began to hum with magic, like a heartbeat or electrical current. Every twenty seconds, a pulse passed through them. The floors were spotless, impossibly clean.

The path split again into three identical corridors. The other two groups split off, leaving us alone as we went right yet again.

As we moved deeper, something changed.  
The echoes stretched unnaturally.  
The air felt disturbed.  
Marks on the ground suggested movement.

Then the corridor opened into something massive.

Rows upon rows of towering constructs and machines stretched into darkness. The ceiling and walls were so far away they disappeared entirely. It felt less like a ruin and more like a functioning facility abandoned by whatever once lived here.

We found a dropped pack near one of the towers. Supplies were scattered. Nearby, a bloodstain had spread outward in a clean cone, long dried.

When we looked back, the entrance was gone. Replaced by smooth, unbroken stone.

---

Inside the pack were notes from the original expedition.

They had been mapping the facility but couldn’t open the containers lining the racks. Early entries were controlled. By day three, they believed they were not alone.

The final notes were frantic:

It reacts to sound, not vision.  
The containers are not storage.  
Something inside one of them was breathing.  
They resealed it.  
They had made a mistake.  
They're the problem and it's removing them.
It moves through the walls.  
Stay quiet.

---

Tension set in immediately.

[[Demingo Indigo|Demingo]] began using hand signs, but no one else understood. We grouped up tightly.

At the bottom of the pack was a crumpled drawing. A metallic, snake-like body. A rhino beetle head. Six blade-like appendages.
[[Crumpled Drawing of creature Session 1.png]]

[[Aporia]], through magic, agreed. Whatever this was, it likely killed the first group.

[[Orivon]] cut his cloak to dampen the noise of [[Henrik]]’s armor.

---

We moved between the towering racks.

The space became maze-like. Every sound echoed too loudly. The air grew colder. The light behaved strangely, almost shifting.

We passed signs of the previous expedition. A lantern cut apart in haste. A pack left untouched.

Then we heard it.

A scraping sound in the distance.

[[Henrik]] cast Message toward it. The sound stopped instantly. The spell returned to him.

We saw something glowing, stretching across the racks.

[[Aporia]] sent her familiar ahead and saw through its eyes. It was dragging a body. The floor itself cleaned the blood behind it, erasing all trace.

We felt the vibrations through the ground.

---

We found the body.

A goblin. Leather armor. A perfectly clean hole through the chest.

When turned over, it had no face. Just smooth skin.

[[Aporia]] attempted Identify. The spell failed, sending blue ripples outward.

Magic was breaking.

---

We tried to retreat, but the entrance was gone.

Spells continued to fail. Each attempt sent out those same blue ripples.

Then something spoke.

An apparition appeared, speaking ancient Draconic before shifting into Common.

We were not supposed to be here.  
We were unrecognized.  
We would be reassigned.

[[Orivon]] attempted to bluff a new designation: surprise inspection.

Above [[Henrik]], the creature appeared.

It paused. Processed. Then withdrew.

Lights activated, forming a path deeper into the facility.

We followed.

---

We walked for what felt like half an hour.

Something moved above us the entire time.

At one point, we passed a skeletal thing wrapped around a corpse. It opened its mouth and released a gray beam, reducing the body to ash, which then vanished completely.

Eventually, the structure shifted, guiding us into a narrow corridor that opened into a circular chamber.

---

At the center was a cylindrical pool filled with a mist-like liquid.

Inside floated a body.

An elf. Pale, almost purplish. Long ears. White hair. Eyes like galaxies.
[[Elf Found in acid pool Session 1.png]]

The liquid burned like acid. It dissolved metal instantly.

Using rope, manacles, and [[Aporia]]'s mage hand, we dragged the body out, losing the rope and manacles in the process.

After about twenty seconds, she woke.

She vomited thick blue fluid and began coughing.

We cleaned her with magic.

She looked at us. Then at the manacles. Then back at us, confused, almost amused.

There had been symbols on the floor that would have released her.

---

She cast Comprehend Languages in a way none of us recognized.

Her name was [[Ellyanora]]. Head of logistics.

She despised the title. And the gods even more.

From her, we learned the truth:

This was not a ruin. It was a facility.

It stored constructs, not living beings.  
She was the only living person left.  
The snake creature was a security construct.  
Maintenance constructs resembled spiders.

She had entered stasis after losing contact with her people during an attack by the gods. She had placed the facility into a defensive sleep state.

She did not know how much time had passed.

Her people had created a weapon to defend themselves from the gods.

The gods responded with genocide.

She refused to name her civilization.

---

Aldric observed her carefully.

She was not a fighter. Her hands were soft. But she demonstrated power far beyond us, casually casting Fabricate repeatedly, forming clothing and tools from nothing.

She was immune to the acid.

She had declared all intruders enemies.

Which meant she had killed the rest of the expedition.

---

Despite that, she chose to reward us.

- [[Orivon]] received a mithril scimitar
- [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] received mithril chainmai
- [[Aporia]] was promised magical tattoos
- [[Demingo Indigo|Demingo]] received a scroll of scrying
- [[Henrik]] received a shield

She then asked us to guide her to a nearby settlement. She wanted to learn what had become of the world.

---

We left the facility together.

As we exited, she cast a spell far beyond our understanding. The entrance vanished completely, leaving only smooth stone behind.

Then the light around us shifted.

It gathered.

Took form.

Wings. Weapons. Radiance.

Angels.

Servants of [[Pelor]].

At their head stood a Solar, its presence overwhelming, its light searing.

They looked at us with finality.

We had walked where the world was not meant to remember.  
They had allowed it to remain, out of mercy.  
It was not meant to be awakened.

And now, they would ensure that what we learned would not spread.

[[Orivon]] responded first.

He insulted them.

And that is where the session ended.
