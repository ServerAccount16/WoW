# Backstory --
[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric Solvar]] was not born remarkable.

He was raised along the western coasts of the [[Dwendalian Empire]], in a small settlement that existed more for passing trade than permanence. His father was a dockhand who took work where ships demanded it. His mother kept what little stability the household had, managing coin, food, and the quiet order of daily life. Neither were exceptional, and neither expected their son to be.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] grew as they had lived. Quiet. Capable. Unnoticed.

He learned to work early. Carrying cargo, repairing nets, watching tides. He was strong, but not uniquely so. Reliable, but not valued for anything beyond that. There was no moment that marked him as different. No expectation placed on him to become more.

If anything, he was expected to remain exactly as he was.

When he came of age, the military was simply the next step. Not out of pride or belief, but because it offered structure and a place to belong. [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] enlisted without hesitation, and without ambition.

He was stationed at a coastal fort where discipline had long since faded. The post was quiet, the soldiers untrained, and [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] himself remained unremarkable. He followed orders, stood watch, and passed through each day without distinction.

That changed the day [[Demetria Indigo]] arrived.

She came seeking refuge, pursued by a group of raiders she had angered. The garrison was unprepared. When the attack came, the soldiers faltered. Lines broke quickly, and what should have been a defense became chaos.

In the middle of it, [[Demetria Indigo|Demetria]] was cornered by the raiders’ captain and his second. The captain laughed when [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] stepped forward, calling him weak and insignificant. He spoke as though [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] had already lost.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] did not respond.

He stepped between them.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] stood his ground as the captain closed in on Demetria, placing himself between them without hesitation. The captain laughed again, certain of the outcome. He spoke of weakness, of place, of how men like [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] existed only to fall.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] said nothing.

When the captain struck, [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] met him. Not with desperation, nor with effort, but with something far quieter.

Certainty.

There was no sudden surge, no violent shift. Only the slow realization, as the clash continued, that something about [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] did not change. Not his footing. Not his breathing. Not his gaze.

While the captain pressed harder, [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] did not yield. He did not strain.

He simply advanced.

And with each step, it became clear.

This was not a contest.

It had already been decided.

The captain’s final strike came with all the force he could muster.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] answered it with a single motion.

The blow was clean. Uncontested.

The captain fell.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] stood over him, unchanged, as though nothing had been risked. When he spoke, it was not with pride, nor anger, but simple truth.

"_This was decided the moment you stepped forward._"

The second in command fled.

The fort survived.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] did not remain.

His superiors saw what he had become and feared it. Power that did not answer to command could not be controlled. Rather than reward him, they removed him from service. Officially, it was a discharge. In truth, it was exile.

[[Demetria Indigo|Demetria]] chose to follow him.

For a time, the two traveled together along the coast, moving between ports, villages, and trade roads that mirrored the life [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] had once known. At first, the work was simple. Guarding merchants. Driving off minor threats. Taking coin where it was offered.

Over time, the work changed.

People began to remember them. Not by name at first, but by reputation. Jobs started coming to them directly. Harder work. Riskier contracts. Situations others were unwilling to take.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] did not go looking for these.

He accepted them when they came.

And he finished them.

Demetria remained at his side through it all, not as someone in need of protection, but as someone who chose to stay. Their partnership was not built on dependence, but on understanding. She had seen what he was when it mattered.

That was enough.

It was during these years that [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] met [[Henrik|Henrik]], a former soldier who had once served at the same fort. Their meeting was unplanned, but familiarity came quickly. Where others saw arrogance, [[Henrik|Henrik]] saw something unfinished.

He did not try to break [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]].

He refined him.

[[Henrik|Henrik]] took on a quiet mentor role, teaching through action rather than instruction. He showed [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] when to act, and when to wait. When certainty was enough, and when control mattered more.

They worked together on and off for years. Fighting, training, and surviving. The pattern remained consistent. [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] stood at the front. [[Henrik|Henrik]] ensured he did not stand alone.

[[Aldric Solvar|Aldric]] never became humble.

But he became controlled.

He has not returned to the place he was raised. Whether his parents still live, he does not know. Whether they would recognize what he has become, he has never considered.

That life no longer concerns him.

Now, at the start of this journey, [[Aldric Solvar|Aldric Solvar]] walks forward as he always has. Calm. Unshaken. Certain.

Those who stand with him are protected.

Those who stand against him are corrected.
